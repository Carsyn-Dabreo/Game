# 3D Models for Realistic Post-Apocalyptic Environment

This directory contains the GLTF/GLB models needed for the realistic post-apocalyptic environment.

## Required Models:

### Buildings:
- `ruined_building.glb` - Damaged building with realistic PBR materials

### Vegetation:
- `overgrown_tree.glb` - Tree with vines and overgrown foliage
- `grass_cluster.glb` - Clumps of overgrown grass
- `vine.glb` - Hanging vines for building coverage

### Debris:
- `abandoned_car.glb` - Rusty, damaged car
- `rubble.glb` - Pile of concrete debris
- `broken_pole.glb` - Damaged utility pole
- `damaged_sign.glb` - Bent and weathered sign

### Environment:
- `abandoned_road.glb` - Road surface with cracks, dirt, and puddles

## Model Requirements:
- Low-to-medium polygon count (suitable for WebGL)
- PBR materials (albedo, roughness, normal maps)
- Optimized texture sizes (1024x1024 or smaller)
- Proper UV unwrapping
- Realistic weathering and damage

## Sources:
You can obtain these models from:
- Sketchfab (free models section)
- TurboSquid (free models)
- CGTrader
- Or create your own using Blender

## Important:
Make sure all models have proper PBR materials with:
- Albedo/diffuse maps
- Roughness maps
- Normal maps
- Metallic maps (where applicable)
- Ambient occlusion maps

This will ensure the realistic lighting and material appearance in the scene.
